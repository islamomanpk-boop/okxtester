# Pure Price-Action Multi-Timeframe Structure V1

Status: Initial implementation; automated tests pending
Model: A — pure price action
Timeframes: 1D and 4H

## Purpose

Create causal market-structure labels without EMA, RSI, ADX,
moving averages, or other indicator-based directional filters.

The detector reads OHLC prices only. Extra indicator columns, if
present in an input file, are ignored.

## Confirmed swing rules

A swing high is a candle whose high is strictly greater than the
highs of the three candles immediately before and after it.

A swing low is a candle whose low is strictly lower than the lows
of the three candles immediately before and after it.

Ties do not qualify as pivots.

A pivot at candle j becomes available only after candle j+3 closes.
The pivot price is recorded on that confirmation candle, not
retroactively on candle j.

## Structure classification

Use the latest two confirmed swing highs and latest two confirmed
swing lows.

- BULLISH: latest high is higher than the prior high AND latest low
  is higher than the prior low (HH + HL).
- BEARISH: latest high is lower than the prior high AND latest low
  is lower than the prior low (LH + LL).
- NEUTRAL: insufficient pivots, mixed structure, or neither condition.

Equal swing prices do not qualify as higher highs, higher lows,
lower highs, or lower lows.

This is a deliberately simple first baseline. More advanced
structure states must be separately specified and tested.

## Break of structure

Bullish BOS: a completed candle closes above the latest confirmed
swing-high price, having closed at or below that level on the
previous candle.

Bearish BOS: a completed candle closes below the latest confirmed
swing-low price, having closed at or above that level on the
previous candle.

The swing must already have been confirmed before the crossing
candle. A level crossed before it became confirmed cannot produce
a retroactive BOS.

Only the first crossing of a given confirmed swing level is emitted.
A newly confirmed swing creates a new eligible level.

BOS is a structural event, not by itself an entry signal.

## Time and causality

Input `ts`, when present, is the candle opening timestamp in
milliseconds. The structure availability timestamp is candle open
time plus the timeframe duration.

- 1D duration: 86,400,000 ms.
- 4H duration: 14,400,000 ms.

Higher-timeframe labels must be attached to 15M signals using a
backward as-of join on availability timestamps. Exact matches are
allowed. Future structure timestamps are forbidden.

The 1D and 4H data must be processed independently. Daily and 4H
alignment is evaluated only using labels already available at the
15M signal's close.

## Initial directional permission

- Daily BULLISH + 4H BULLISH: long trend setups permitted.
- Daily BEARISH + 4H BEARISH: short trend setups permitted.
- Any conflict or NEUTRAL state: no trend trade in the initial baseline.

Range trading is a separate experiment and is not enabled by this
trend permission rule.

## Model separation

Model A uses this price-action structure engine.

Model B retains the existing indicator-assisted regime classifier.
Model B results must not be mixed with Model A results.

## Known limitations

- NEUTRAL combines ranges, transitions, and insufficient structure.
- BOS is a close-through event and does not model retests.
- Equal highs/lows are not grouped into liquidity zones.
- No CHoCH state is emitted in this initial version.
- No 15M entry signals or execution are generated by this module.

These limitations are intentional and must not be silently changed
after observing out-of-sample results.
