# Phase 3 sideways-regime research archive V1

These are permanent, version-controlled outputs from the five-asset historical study.
They are generated by the corresponding scripts in `research/` and use the frozen
source datasets already stored in this repository.

- `episode_retest_anatomy_v1/`: event anatomy, forward path outcomes, validation, summaries, metadata.
- `early_path_followthrough_v1/`: sequence labels, post-landmark outcomes, validation, summaries, metadata.
- `sequence_block_bootstrap_v1/`: calendar moving-block bootstrap contrasts and run metadata.
- `pre_event_matched_controls_v1/`: initial 1:1 exact-asset/direction/month matching; retained for audit, but residual covariate imbalance was too large for a conclusive comparison.
- `pre_event_matched_controls_v2/`: very tight per-covariate caliper; retained for audit, but it yielded only one broad pair and no strict pairs.
- `pre_event_matched_controls_v3/`: exact-asset/direction/month matching with a looser per-covariate caliper; retained for audit because pair counts remained small.
- `pre_event_matched_controls_v4/`: 1:1 matching by exact asset and direction, with a 30-day maximum time gap and pre-event covariate calipers. This yielded 71 broad and 45 strict pairs. Some residual standardized mean differences remain, especially the signed close-to-boundary distance, so interpret as sensitivity evidence rather than a fully balanced causal comparison.

The sequence-class contrasts are observational associations, not causal estimates
or proof of a profitable trading strategy. Bootstrap intervals are not adjusted for
multiple comparisons, and overlapping events may remain dependent. Broad and strict
cohorts are nested, not independent. Review each run's metadata and covariate-balance
tables before interpreting outcome contrasts.

- `asset_robustness_v1/`: per-asset and leave-one-asset-out robustness for V4 matched pairs, using 10,000-replicate 3/7/14-day calendar moving-block bootstrap intervals. V1 includes 71 broad pairs and 45 strict pairs. Broad pair counts by asset: BTC 16, ETH 14, SOL 15, DOGE 14, AVAX 12; strict: BTC 11, ETH 12, SOL 6, DOGE 8, AVAX 8. The broad 1h direction-adjusted close and inward-excursion contrasts, and broad 4h inward-excursion contrast, are positive in the pooled analysis; the strict 1h close/inward and 4h inward contrasts are also positive but smaller. The 24h pooled intervals are wide and include zero for the close and inward metrics; outward-penetration contrasts are less consistent by horizon/cohort. Leave-one-asset-out results show that the main 1h inward-excursion association is not driven by a single asset, while some longer-horizon and close/outward results weaken when individual assets are omitted. Per-asset samples are small (6–16 pairs), so their intervals are often wide. Treat all findings as exploratory observational associations, not causal effects or evidence of a tradable edge. The analysis does not remove V4's residual covariate imbalance, uses nested broad/strict cohorts, and makes many unadjusted comparisons.


- `pre_event_matched_controls_v5/`: outcome-blind target-caliper sensitivity grid around `signed_close_boundary_distance_width` (0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00 robust-SD units), retaining V4's exact asset/direction strata, 30-day maximum timestamp gap, RMS distance limit 2.0, and overall per-covariate limit 1.5. This study does not load or inspect outcomes. Broad matching retained 21/35/46/56 pairs across the four thresholds; at 0.75 the target-covariate absolute SMD was 0.230 and the maximum absolute SMD across all covariates was 0.230. At 0.25, the target SMD improved to 0.028 but only 21 pairs remained and the maximum other-covariate SMD was 0.320. Strict matching retained 11/20/29/34 pairs; at 0.50 the target SMD was 0.139 and the maximum absolute SMD was 0.300. The strict 0.25 setting retained only 11 pairs and still had a maximum SMD of 0.594. There is no single threshold that clearly optimizes pair retention and balance for both cohorts. These are balance diagnostics only; V5 makes no outcome comparison and does not establish exchangeability or causality. Month is recorded for audit but is not a matching stratum.

Support and resistance will be analysed separately before any pooling; long/short and boundary asymmetries must remain visible. ### Actual local funding coverage audit — 2026-11-25

The real-data audit was run in the user's Termux environment against the five processed OKX `realizedRate` CSVs. It **PASSED**: 1,188 rows per instrument (5,940 total), identical coverage from 2025-10-20 00:00 UTC through 2026-11-19 16:00 UTC, expected 8-hour cadence, zero missing settlements, no gaps/irregular intervals, and matching timestamp sets across all five assets. SHA-256 hashes and exact local source paths are preserved in:
- `funding_coverage_audit_v1/funding_coverage_audit.md`
- `funding_coverage_audit_v1/funding_coverage_manifest.json`

The raw/processed CSVs remain local and are not committed. The manifest records their hashes so the audited inputs can be identified. This closes the current-file-availability and coverage-evidence items (G0-01/G0-02) for that execution environment only. It does **not** verify funding cashflow application, mark-price provenance, or fail-closed engine behaviour; Gate 0 remains CONDITIONAL HOLD and strategy backtesting remains unauthorized.
